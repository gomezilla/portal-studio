A quick note about account emails and exports

A couple of helpful details for the online version.

Signup and password-reset emails

Online accounts use Supabase’s free-tier email service. Verification and password-reset messages may be delayed or rate-limited. Check your spam folder, and wait a little before requesting another email.

ZIP export limits

To discourage abuse and keep the shared service available for everyone, exports are limited per account. New accounts start with three exports; use Request more access if you need an increase.

PORTAL STUDIO

Everything you need to shape your Omada portal

Explore the design, preview, saving, and export tools available to portal creators.

CONTENT

Customize portal content

Edit the portal name, welcome headline and subheading, gallery captions, access labels, speed-test text, plans table, connection instructions, notes, and footer.

LAYOUT

Choose what appears

Show or hide the header, photo gallery, speed-test card, plans, connection guide, note, and footer. Omada’s access method stays enabled.

STYLE

Start with a visual preset

Choose Material You, Facebook, Social Media, YouTube, TikTok, Gaming / Neon, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Midnight, or Rose. Fine-tune the brand color, light/dark/device color mode, and corner style.

IMAGES

Manage gallery and guide images

Add, replace, or remove gallery slides. Replace or remove the sample image for each connection-guide step. Images appear in the preview and are included in exports when kept.

PREVIEW

Preview as visitors will see it

See edits update live in a mobile-sized preview by default. Switch to desktop width and check light or dark appearance without changing the saved portal theme.

SPEED TEST

Configure the optional network check

Enable or disable the live upload/download test and ISP lookup. If enabled, add speed.cloudflare.com and ipwho.is to Omada’s Pre-authentication Access Control list.

DRAFTS

Save and manage your work

Sign in with email and password to save private drafts, reopen them from the home page, and change or reset your password. New accounts start with one saved portal and three exports; request more access for approval.

EXPORT

Export Omada-ready ZIP files

Check the estimated ZIP size while editing. Exports must stay under 2 MB; if they exceed the limit, replace images with smaller files or remove gallery images. The standard Omada portal files are retained.

FLEXIBLE

Designed for phones and desktops

The editor adapts to smaller screens, though a laptop or desktop is recommended for the most comfortable editing experience. Support and optional donation information are available from the top menu.
